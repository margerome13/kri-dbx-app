Maker guideSubmit KRI

Maker

You enter KRI values for your department only. A Checker approves or rejects; you do not approve your own submissions.

What you see in the app

Sidebar: Submit KRI → Submit / Edit KRI Value.

Four tabs:

  • Submit / edit — enter or update values
  • My submissions — everything you’ve submitted
  • My approved — submissions a Checker approved
  • My rejections — submissions sent back to you (read the Checker’s reason)

How to submit or edit a KRI

  1. Open Submit / edit.
  2. Choose Entity (e.g. Maya PH). Your Department is fixed to the one RCO assigned you.
  3. Choose the KRI and the reporting month (any day in that month is fine; the app uses the whole month).
  4. Enter the Actual value in the format shown (depends on the KRI — %, days, duration, etc.).
  5. RAG (Green / Amber / Red) is calculated for you from the value and the KRI’s thresholds — you don’t pick it yourself in normal cases.
  6. If RAG is Amber or Red, fill in Remarks (cause, actions, target date).
  7. Click Save submission. Status becomes Submitted and waits for your Checker.

After you save

Submitted Waiting for Checker. You can still edit until they act.
Rejected Checker returned it — read their reason on the form or under My rejections, fix the value/remarks, save again. It goes back to Submitted.
Approved Locked for you. You cannot edit. Contact RCO Admin if a correction is needed.

Do’s

  • Use the reporting month that matches your KRI’s cadence (monthly, quarterly, etc.).
  • Double-check Actual value format (e.g. 75%, whole days, 1:30:00 for duration KRIs).
  • Add clear remarks whenever RAG is Amber or Red.
  • Use My rejections to see Checker feedback before you resubmit.

Don’ts

  • Don’t expect to edit an Approved submission — only Admin can correct those.
  • Don’t submit for another department — you only see your assigned department.
  • Don’t pick a department or KRI that isn’t in the catalog yet; if nothing appears, ask RCO to Add a KRI for your entity + department first.
  • Don’t ignore rejection reasons — fix and resubmit so the Checker can approve.

If something’s wrong

  • No KRIs listed — RCO may not have defined Active KRIs for your entity + department yet.
  • Wrong department on your profile — ask RCO Admin to update User Role Manager.
  • Can’t open the app — you may need Databricks Share access in addition to a Maker role.
