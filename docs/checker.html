Checker

You approve or reject submissions from Makers in your department. You do not change their Actual value or remarks — that keeps maker–checker separation clear.

What you see in the app

Sidebar: Review → Review Submitted KRIs.

Three tabs:

  • Pending review — submissions waiting for you (Submitted status)
  • My approvals — KRIs you approved
  • My rejections — KRIs you rejected (with your reasons)

How to review a submission

  1. Open Pending review.
  2. Select a submission from the list (entity, department, KRI, period, Maker).
  3. Read the Maker’s Actual value, RAG, and remarks.
  4. Choose one:
    • Approve — optional note for the record. Submission becomes Approved and is locked for the Maker.
    • Reject — you must enter a reason. Maker can fix and resubmit.

Do’s

  • Review only what Makers submitted — value, RAG, and remarks are read-only for you.
  • Give clear, actionable rejection reasons (what to fix or what’s missing).
  • Work through Pending review regularly so Submitted items don’t pile up.
  • Use My approvals / My rejections to audit what you’ve already decided.

Don’ts

  • Don’t try to “fix” the Maker’s numbers in the app — reject with guidance or ask Admin if a catalog/threshold issue exists.
  • Don’t reject without a reason — the form requires it.
  • Don’t expect to see other departments — your queue is scoped to your assigned department.
  • Don’t use Submit KRI or Manage Existing KRIs — those are not part of the Checker role.

If something’s wrong

  • Department not set on your profile — ask RCO Admin to set it in User Role Manager.
  • Empty queue — no Submitted items for your department, or Makers haven’t submitted yet.
  • Wrong thresholds or KRI definition — escalate to RCO Admin (catalog changes), not by editing the submission.
